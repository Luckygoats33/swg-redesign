The SWG Method

Five steps. One sequence.

Every engagement follows the same discipline so the work stays connected to the real constraint.

Step 01
Diagnose

Review the business stage, growth goal, offer clarity, customer path, team structure, and tech stack. Understand what is actually happening before recommending anything.

Step 02
Sequence

Identify what is blocking growth and put the fixes in the right order. Not everything needs to happen at once. The sequence determines the speed.

Step 03
Scope

Define the build: what to fix first, what to build next, what should wait. Set expectations, timelines, and deliverables before work begins.

Step 04
Build

Execute the work: website, CRM, AI, marketing, reporting, ops. SWG handles the implementation so the team can stay focused on running the business.

Step 05
Operate

Stay with the company to optimize, report, and adapt. Systems only work if someone is watching them. SWG provides the ongoing rhythm to keep things moving.

The business review

What the business review examines.

Ten areas. One conversation. The review maps the full picture so nothing critical gets missed.

Business Stage

Where the company sits today: startup, growth, scale, or transition. The stage shapes the priority.

Growth Goal

Revenue target, timeline, and what success looks like. Aligns the plan to something measurable.

Offer Clarity

Can a visitor understand what the company sells, who it is for, and why it matters in under ten seconds?

Customer Path

How a lead finds the company, evaluates the offer, and becomes a customer. Where the path breaks.

Sales Process

How leads are contacted, followed up, and closed. Whether the process is documented or improvised.

Team Ownership

Who owns marketing, sales, ops, and tech. Where roles overlap, gaps exist, or the founder fills in.

Tech Stack

What tools are in place, whether they are connected, and what is working versus what is just costing money.

Marketing Effectiveness

Which channels produce results, which are noise, and whether spend is connected to revenue.

AI Opportunities

Where automation, AI workflows, or intelligent tooling could save time, reduce error, or unlock capacity.

Reporting Quality

Whether the company has a clear view of performance, or whether reporting is manual, late, or misleading.

Deliverables

What you receive after the review.

Four deliverables. Each one designed to give you clarity, sequence, and a plan you can act on.

01

Constraint Map

Where growth is actually leaking. Not a list of everything that could be better, but a clear picture of the constraints that matter right now.

02

Priority Sequence

What to fix first and why. The sequence is based on impact, dependency, and what the team can absorb. Not everything happens at once.

03

Build Scope

What to build, in what order, and with what resources. Timelines, deliverables, and ownership defined before work begins.

04

Operating Rhythm

How to review, report, and adjust going forward. A recurring cadence that keeps systems accountable and decisions data-driven.
