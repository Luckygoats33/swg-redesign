Situation

CamoToe is a direct-to-consumer men’s cosmetic brand selling a matte toenail concealer through Shopify. The founder had a product and a live store, but no functioning ad infrastructure, no email program, and no conversion-optimized site. The brand needed to go from “store exists” to “customers are buying” across every digital channel simultaneously.

Constraint

The site, ad copy, and product metadata were saturated with medical and drug-claim language that violated Google Ads, Meta, and FTC guidelines. Nothing could launch until every customer-facing surface was audited and rewritten. Budget was limited to roughly $1,000/month across all paid channels.

Work

Full compliance audit identifying 30+ drug-claim violations. Rewrote all SEO metadata and product copy with compliant language. Built and launched Meta Ads (8 ads, 3 ad sets). Created geo-targeted Google Ads Search campaign for Dubai beach season (5 ad groups, 27 keywords). Klaviyo email flow audit: 21 emails reviewed, 79 fixes deployed. Produced 100+ AI-generated ad creatives. Set up Google Merchant Center, GA4 tracking, and automated budget controls.

Result

3.95% CTR on Meta ads in the first 72 hours (industry average 1-2%). Top ad achieved 6.34% CTR at $0.58 CPC. $1.49 average CPC across all ad sets. 9,117 impressions and 201 link clicks in 3 days. Zero compliance violations remaining. Site speed: A+ (45ms TTFB, 400ms FCP). Complete advertising infrastructure built from zero.
