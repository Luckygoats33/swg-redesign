DTC E-Commerce

CamoToe — DTC Men’s Grooming Brand

Situation

CamoToe is a direct-to-consumer men’s cosmetic brand selling a matte toenail concealer through Shopify. The founder had a product and a live store, but no functioning ad infrastructure, no email program, and no conversion-optimized site. The brand needed to go from “store exists” to “customers are buying” across every digital channel simultaneously.

Constraint

The site, ad copy, and product metadata were saturated with medical and drug-claim language that violated Google Ads, Meta, and FTC guidelines. Nothing could launch until every customer-facing surface was audited and rewritten. Budget was limited to roughly $1,000/month across all paid channels.

Work

Full compliance audit identifying 30+ drug-claim violations. Rewrote all SEO metadata and product copy with compliant language. Built and launched Meta Ads (8 ads, 3 ad sets). Created geo-targeted Google Ads Search campaign for Dubai beach season (5 ad groups, 27 keywords). Klaviyo email flow audit: 21 emails reviewed, 79 fixes deployed. Produced 100+ AI-generated ad creatives. Set up Google Merchant Center, GA4 tracking, and automated budget controls.

Result

3.95% CTR on Meta ads in the first 72 hours (industry average 1-2%). Top ad achieved 6.34% CTR at $0.58 CPC. $1.49 average CPC across all ad sets. 9,117 impressions and 201 link clicks in 3 days. Zero compliance violations remaining. Site speed: A+ (45ms TTFB, 400ms FCP). Complete advertising infrastructure built from zero.

B2B Professional Services

Meridian Partners — B2B Consulting Firm

Situation

A 12-person management consulting firm where the two founding partners personally generated 90% of new business through their networks and conference speaking. No inbound pipeline, no CRM discipline, and a website that had not been updated in three years. Junior consultants had no way to contribute to business development.

Constraint

The partners resisted anything that felt like “marketing” — they built the firm on relationships and referrals. Any system had to feel like a natural extension of their existing approach, not a bolt-on marketing machine. Budget was capped at $15K total.

Work

Diagnosed the founder bottleneck as the core growth constraint. Redesigned the website around 6 service-specific landing pages. Built a CRM workflow in HubSpot replacing a shared spreadsheet. Created a thought-leadership content system repurposed across LinkedIn, email, and conference proposals. Trained 3 junior consultants on relationship-based business development.

Result

Inbound leads increased from 2/month to 11/month within 4 months. Junior consultants sourced 3 closed deals ($340K combined). Time-to-close dropped from 47 days to 29 days. Website contact form submissions increased 6x. Founders reduced BD time from ~15 hours/week each to ~6 hours/week. $480K in new ARR within the engagement period.

Health & Wellness DTC

Vitality Roots — Health & Wellness DTC Brand

Situation

Vitality Roots sells plant-based supplement powders through Shopify with a loyal but small customer base. The founder and a single operations manager handled everything — customer service, fulfillment, social media, email, and ads. They were spending 60+ hours per week on execution with no time to think about growth. Repeat purchase rate was declining.

Constraint

The founder had been burned by a previous agency that ran a $4,000/month retainer for 6 months with no measurable improvement. Trust was low. She insisted on understanding every system before approving it — no black-box solutions. She refused to increase ad spend until existing spend was proven efficient.

Work

Mapped every operational task, identifying 22 hours/week of automatable work. Built AI-powered customer service triage. Restructured Meta ad account from 1 bloated campaign into a clean 3-campaign architecture. Audited and rebuilt 7 Klaviyo email flows. Created a subscription-first landing page. Set up weekly automated performance reporting and inventory-based ad pausing.

Result

Founder’s weekly hours dropped from 62 to 38 within 8 weeks. Email revenue increased 74% ($8,200/month to $14,300/month). Meta ROAS improved from 1.8x to 3.4x on the same $2,500/month spend. Repeat purchase rate reversed from 14% declining to 22% and climbing. Subscribe & Save enrollment grew from 3% to 11% of orders.

Multi-Location HVAC Services

Apex Mechanical — Local Multi-Location Service Business

Situation

Apex Mechanical operates 4 HVAC and plumbing locations across Dallas-Fort Worth. Each location had its own website, Google Business Profile, and ad hoc lead handling — some used a shared email inbox, one used a whiteboard. The owner had no unified view of which locations were profitable or how many leads were being dropped.

Constraint

All four locations had to keep running on existing systems while new infrastructure was built alongside. The 28 field technicians ranged from tech-comfortable to tech-resistant — any new system that added friction would be rejected. Total technology budget was $1,200/month.

Work

Consolidated 4 websites into a single multi-location site with location-specific landing pages. Implemented ServiceTitan across all locations with standardized workflows. Migrated 11,400 customer records. Optimized all 4 Google Business Profiles. Built Google Ads campaigns with location-specific budgets and seasonal bid adjustments. Created a single-screen operations dashboard.

Result

Lead response time dropped from 4.2 hours to 18 minutes. Identified Location 3 losing money on every Google Ads click due to misconfigured service area — fixed in week one, saving $1,400/month. Lead volume increased 31% in 90 days. Close rate improved from 34% to 48%. Google reviews grew from 187 to 340 in 4 months.

B2B SaaS

Archway Labs — Tech Startup Post-Failed-Agency

Situation

Archway Labs is a 15-person B2B SaaS company selling project management software to construction firms. After closing a $2M seed round, they hired a performance marketing agency at $8,500/month. After 5 months and $42,500 in agency fees (plus $61,000 in ad spend), they had 23 trial signups and zero converted customers from paid channels.

Constraint

The founders were out of patience and nearly out of runway for marketing experimentation. They needed directional results within 30 days or they were pulling all paid spend. The previous agency left behind a Google Ads account with 847 keywords, 12 campaigns, and no conversion tracking.

Work

Forensic audit of the previous agency’s account: broad match keywords with no negatives, no conversion tracking, campaigns optimized for clicks not conversions, traffic sent to the homepage instead of landing pages. Paused all 12 campaigns, archived 790 keywords, rebuilt with 3 tightly themed campaigns. Built 3 dedicated landing pages. Set up LinkedIn Ads pilot. Restructured trial onboarding from 2 generic emails to a 7-email construction-specific series.

Result

Cost per trial dropped from $2,652 to $187 within 45 days. 34 qualified trial signups in 60 days on $4,200/month spend (vs. 23 in 5 months at $12,200/month). 6 trials converted to paying customers ($14,400 ARR each). LinkedIn pilot: 18 demo requests at $94 CPL. Trial-to-paid conversion rate increased from 4% to 19%.

The forensic audit of our previous agency’s work was eye-opening. SWG found $1,400 per month in wasted ad spend in the first week alone.

Marcus Rivera, Owner, Apex Mechanical
