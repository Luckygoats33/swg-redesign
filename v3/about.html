Why the model exists.

Most agencies and consultants sell services in isolation. A website from one vendor, paid media from another, a CRM implementation from a third. Each one operates independently, measures success differently, and has no visibility into what the others are doing. The business owner is left to connect the pieces.

SWG was built because that model fails growing companies. The companies we work with do not need more services. They need someone to start with the business itself: what is actually broken, what is blocking growth, and what needs to happen in what order.

The model came from seeing companies waste hundreds of thousands of dollars on disconnected work. Websites that launched but did not convert. CRMs that were implemented but never adopted. Marketing campaigns that ran but could not be measured. The problem was never the individual service. The problem was that no one started with the diagnosis.

Who we work with.

Founder-led companies

You built this business from scratch. You know the product, the customers, and the market better than anyone. But your growth has outpaced your systems, and you are still the bottleneck on too many decisions.

We work with founder-led companies across DTC, SaaS, professional services, and healthcare.

Owner-led companies

You acquired or inherited a business with real revenue. The team is in place, but the infrastructure is not. You need modernized operations, updated technology, and a strategy that connects marketing to revenue.

Whether you acquired, inherited, or grew into ownership, we help modernize without disrupting what works.

Operator-led companies

You run the business day-to-day. You see the disconnects between sales, marketing, and operations, but fixing them requires expertise and bandwidth your team does not have.

We give operators the tools and systems to fix what they already see is broken.

Leadership-driven teams

Your leadership team is aligned on where the company needs to go. What you need is an external partner who can diagnose, plan, and execute without adding more complexity to your internal structure.

Our model is designed to plug in alongside your leadership team, not create another reporting layer.

What we believe.

  1. 01 Diagnose before building. Every engagement starts with understanding what is actually broken, not what the client thinks they want to buy.
  2. 02 Sequence matters more than speed. Doing the right thing second is better than doing the wrong thing first. Order of operations determines outcomes.
  3. 03 Tools do not fix unclear ownership. A CRM does not help if no one is responsible for updating it. Technology only works when process and accountability exist first.
  4. 04 AI needs process, inputs, review, and approval. AI is a multiplier, not a replacement. It works when it is embedded in a workflow with human oversight at every checkpoint.
  5. 05 Growth needs an operating rhythm. Strategy decks gather dust. What works is a recurring cadence of review, adjustment, and accountability built into how the company runs.
  6. 06 Strategy without execution is a document. The plan only matters if it gets built, launched, measured, and improved. Execution is the strategy.
