What you ask for vs. what we usually fix

The first request is often tactical. The work underneath is where growth gets unstuck.

Website request

"We need the site to finally match the company."

Usually means

Positioning, offer clarity, proof, service architecture, conversion paths, and analytics need to catch up.

Paid media request

"We are spending, but it is not converting."

Usually means

The audience, landing page, offer, tracking, follow-up, and sales handoff are not working as one path.

AI request

"We need AI automation."

Usually means

The process, inputs, review rules, owner, and human approval path need to be defined before tools help.

CRM request

"Our CRM is messy."

Usually means

Stages, ownership, source tracking, follow-up rules, pipeline hygiene, and reporting are not agreed on.

Marketing request

"We are active everywhere, but nothing compounds."

Usually means

Content, email, social, SEO, paid media, proof capture, and campaign measurement need one operating rhythm.

Operations request

"The team is still doing too much by hand."

Usually means

Intake, approvals, handoffs, content review, reporting, and accountability need cleaner workflows and automation.

Rescue request

"We already spent five or six figures and still do not have something usable."

Usually means

The build needs a calm audit: what failed, what can be salvaged, what should be rebuilt, and what the next decision should be.
