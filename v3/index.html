Common situations

Companies usually come to SWG when...

Usually means

The site was built for a past version of the business. Brand, messaging, and conversion paths need to match current goals.

Usually means

Contacts, deals, and automations have accumulated without structure. The team works around the CRM instead of through it.

Usually means

Tools were adopted without a workflow. AI works when it is embedded in existing processes with clear inputs and outputs.

Usually means

There is no operating system: no dashboards, no documented workflows, no delegation layer. The founder is the glue.

Usually means

The ads may be fine. The landing pages, offer structure, or post-click experience is where the conversion breaks.

Usually means

Content, email, SEO, and paid are running in parallel but not feeding each other. No system turns effort into momentum.
