Problems we solve

The problem is rarely just one service.

Companies usually come to SWG when growth is no longer a marketing problem alone. The real issue is almost always structural.

Start with the business review

Trusted by growing companies across 30+ industries

01

We need the site to finally match the company.

The website no longer matches the company. It was built for an earlier stage and now misrepresents the brand, the offer, or the customer path.

What SWG Reviews
  • Brand positioning vs. current offer
  • Conversion path clarity
  • Page structure and hierarchy
  • Mobile experience quality
What May Need to Be Built
  • Full site redesign or key page rebuild
  • Messaging and positioning overhaul
  • Conversion-focused landing pages
  • SEO foundation and content structure
02

We are spending, but it is not converting.

Paid media is not converting. The issue is rarely the ads alone. It is usually the landing page, the offer, the funnel, or the tracking.

What SWG Reviews
  • Ad-to-landing-page alignment
  • Conversion tracking and attribution
  • Offer clarity and pricing structure
  • Funnel completeness and drop-off
What May Need to Be Built
  • Landing page rebuild with conversion focus
  • Tracking and pixel infrastructure
  • Campaign restructuring by angle
  • Post-click nurture sequences
03

Our CRM is messy.

The CRM needs cleanup. Contacts are duplicated, segments are undefined, automations are broken, and no one trusts the data enough to act on it.

What SWG Reviews
  • Data hygiene and duplication
  • Segmentation and tagging logic
  • Automation and flow health
  • Reporting accuracy
What May Need to Be Built
  • Full CRM cleanup and deduplication
  • Segmentation framework and tags
  • Lifecycle automations and triggers
  • Dashboards tied to actual revenue
04

We need AI automation.

AI ideas are stalled. The team has heard about what is possible but has no plan, no prioritization, and no one to build or integrate it.

What SWG Reviews
  • Repetitive tasks and manual bottlenecks
  • Data availability and quality
  • Current tool stack compatibility
  • Team readiness and adoption risk
What May Need to Be Built
  • AI workflow scoping and prioritization
  • Automated intake, routing, or triage
  • AI-assisted content or reporting tools
  • Custom integrations and prompt systems
05

The founder is still the operating system.

The founder carries too much. Every decision, every client, every fire runs through one person. Growth stalls because the business cannot scale past the founder's bandwidth.

What SWG Reviews
  • Decision flow and bottlenecks
  • Team roles and ownership gaps
  • Process documentation status
  • Delegation readiness
What May Need to Be Built
  • Operational playbooks and SOPs
  • Role clarity and accountability maps
  • Reporting that replaces check-ins
  • Systems that run without the founder
06

We are active everywhere, but nothing compounds.

Marketing is not compounding. The company is posting, emailing, running ads, and attending events, but none of it builds on itself. There is activity without accumulation.

What SWG Reviews
  • Channel performance and ROI
  • Content strategy and repurposing
  • Audience growth and engagement trends
  • Attribution across touchpoints
What May Need to Be Built
  • Unified marketing strategy with priorities
  • Content engine with compounding structure
  • Channel reduction and focus plan
  • Marketing calendar tied to revenue goals
07

The team is doing work by hand.

Too much manual work. Tasks that should take minutes take hours. Data moves between systems by copy-paste. Reports are assembled manually every week.

What SWG Reviews
  • Workflow mapping and time audits
  • Tool stack gaps and redundancies
  • Data flow between systems
  • Automation opportunity sizing
What May Need to Be Built
  • Automated data pipelines and syncs
  • Workflow automation across tools
  • Automated reporting and dashboards
  • Integration layer between core systems
08

The last tech build needs a rescue.

A prior build failed. The company invested in a website, app, CRM migration, or automation project that was never finished, never worked right, or was abandoned by the vendor.

What SWG Reviews
  • What was built vs. what was scoped
  • What is salvageable vs. needs replacing
  • Root cause of the failure
  • Current state and technical debt
What May Need to Be Built
  • Recovery plan with realistic scope
  • Rebuild of failed components
  • Vendor transition and knowledge transfer
  • Testing and QA before relaunch
