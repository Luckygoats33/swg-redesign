### CamoToe -- DTC Men's Grooming Brand

**Industry:** Consumer products / Men's personal care (DTC e-commerce)
**Revenue Range:** Pre-revenue to early-stage ($0-500K)
**Engagement Duration:** 4+ months (ongoing)

**Situation:** CamoToe is a direct-to-consumer men's cosmetic brand selling a matte toenail concealer through Shopify. The founder had a product and a live store, but no functioning ad infrastructure, no email program, and no conversion-optimized site. The brand needed to go from "store exists" to "customers are buying" across every digital channel simultaneously.

**Constraint:** The site, ad copy, and product metadata were saturated with medical and drug-claim language -- terms like "antifungal," "treats fungus," and "FDA-recognized" that violated Google Ads healthcare policy, Meta ad policy, and FTC guidelines. Nothing could launch until every customer-facing surface was audited and rewritten with compliant cosmetic-only language. Budget was limited to roughly $1,000/month across all paid channels.

**Work Performed:**
- Full compliance audit across the Shopify store, identifying 30+ drug-claim violations in product titles, meta tags, OG descriptions, FAQ sections, structured data markup, and navigation menus
- Rewrote all SEO metadata, product descriptions, and landing page copy using compliant cosmetic language while preserving search intent
- SEO/performance audit (12-point fix list): corrected H1 structure, trimmed meta descriptions, fixed OG tags, identified render-blocking CSS, and recommended Product schema markup
- Built and launched Meta Ads campaign: 8 ads across 3 ad sets (Immediate Result, Confidence, Social Pain) targeting men 28-65 in warm-climate US states and international beach markets
- Created geo-targeted Google Ads Search campaign for Dubai beach season: 5 ad groups, 27 keywords, 43 negative keywords, all with compliant copy
- Rewrote the entire Google Ads plan from scratch after the original campaign scripts were found to contain policy-bypass functions that risked account suspension
- Klaviyo email flow audit: reviewed 21 emails, deployed 79 individual fixes across welcome, abandoned cart, and post-purchase sequences
- Built first-ever email blast calendar (4 monthly sends, June-August) for a 242-subscriber list with seasonal angles and discount codes
- Produced 100+ AI-generated ad creative images across 10 categories using Higgsfield, with contact sheets and A/B tier grading
- Planned 64-clip founder video shoot across 7 locations with scripted hooks, FAQ responses, and b-roll shot lists
- Set up Google Merchant Center (16 products approved), GA4 tracking, and conversion tracking through the Shopify Google channel
- Configured budget management scripts and scheduled tasks for automated daily spend controls

**Results:**
- 3.95% click-through rate on Meta ads in the first 72 hours (industry average for DTC is 1-2%)
- Top-performing ad achieved 6.34% CTR at $0.58 CPC
- $1.49 average CPC across all ad sets, with the best ad set delivering $0.63 CPC
- 9,117 impressions and 201 link clicks in the first 3 days of spend
- Identified critical product page conversion gap (130 product views, 0 add-to-cart) -- diagnosing the funnel leak before wasting further ad spend
- Zero compliance violations remaining in any live ad or landing page after the rewrite
- Site speed score: A+ (45ms TTFB, 400ms FCP, 1.3s full load, 0.009 CLS)
- Complete advertising infrastructure built from zero: Meta, Google Search, Google Shopping, email, and creative pipeline all operational

**Timeline:** Ongoing engagement. Compliance audit and site cleanup: 2 weeks. Meta Ads launch: 1 week after cleanup. Google Ads (Dubai Search): launched same week. Klaviyo audit and email calendar: completed in a single session. Creative production: ongoing.

### Meridian Partners -- B2B Professional Services Firm

**Industry:** Management consulting / Professional services
**Revenue Range:** $3M annual revenue
**Engagement Duration:** 6 months

**Situation:** Meridian Partners is a 12-person management consulting firm where the two founding partners personally generated 90% of new business through their networks and conference speaking. The firm had no inbound pipeline, no CRM discipline, and a website that had not been updated in three years. Junior consultants had no way to contribute to business development, and the founders were burning out as the sole revenue engine.

**Constraint:** The partners resisted anything that felt like "marketing" -- they built the firm on relationships and referrals, and viewed outbound campaigns and social media as beneath their brand. Any system had to feel like a natural extension of their existing relationship-building, not a bolt-on marketing machine. Budget for the engagement was capped at $15K total.

**Work Performed:**
- Diagnosed the founder bottleneck as the core growth constraint -- not a website or marketing problem, but a business model problem
- Redesigned the website around 6 service-specific landing pages with clear positioning, outcome language, and inbound conversion paths (contact forms, PDF downloads, consultation booking)
- Built a lightweight CRM workflow in HubSpot replacing the founders' shared spreadsheet: deal stages, activity logging, and automated follow-up reminders
- Created a "thought leadership" content system: one monthly long-form article repurposed into LinkedIn posts, email newsletter, and conference proposal abstracts
- Trained 3 junior consultants on relationship-based business development using the CRM pipeline, giving them visibility into where opportunities stalled
- Implemented basic lead scoring so the founders only saw qualified inbound inquiries, not every form submission
- Set up monthly reporting: pipeline value, conversion rates by source, and time-to-close benchmarks

**Results:**
- Inbound leads increased from 2/month to 11/month within 4 months
- Junior consultants sourced 3 closed deals ($340K combined) in the first two quarters -- revenue the founders did not personally generate
- Average time-to-close dropped from 47 days to 29 days with CRM follow-up automation
- Website contact form submissions increased 6x after service page redesign
- Founders reduced business development time from ~15 hours/week each to ~6 hours/week
- Firm added $480K in new annual recurring revenue within the engagement period

**Timeline:** 6 months. Discovery and strategy: 3 weeks. Website and CRM: 6 weeks. Content system and training: ongoing through month 6.

**Quote:** "We came in thinking we needed a new website. What we actually needed was a system that let our team sell without us standing in every room. The website was just one piece of that."

### Vitality Roots -- Health & Wellness DTC Brand

**Industry:** Health & wellness supplements (DTC e-commerce)
**Revenue Range:** $1.2M annual revenue
**Engagement Duration:** 4 months

**Situation:** Vitality Roots sells plant-based supplement powders through Shopify with a loyal but small customer base. The founder and a single operations manager handled everything -- customer service, fulfillment, social media, email marketing, and ad management. They were spending 60+ hours per week on execution and had no time to think about growth. Their ad account was running but unmonitored, email flows had not been updated in 8 months, and repeat purchase rate was declining.

**Constraint:** The founder had been burned by a previous agency that ran a $4,000/month retainer for 6 months with no measurable improvement. Trust was low, and she insisted on understanding every system before approving it. No black-box solutions, no "just trust us" dashboards. She also refused to increase ad spend until existing spend was proven efficient.

**Work Performed:**
- Mapped every operational task the founder and ops manager performed weekly, identifying 22 hours/week of repetitive work eligible for automation
- Built AI-powered customer service triage: automated responses for shipping inquiries, order status, and return requests, escalating only complex issues to the founder
- Restructured Meta ad account from 1 bloated campaign with 14 ad sets into a clean 3-campaign architecture (prospecting, retargeting, retention) with proper exclusion audiences
- Audited and rebuilt 7 Klaviyo email flows: welcome series, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, replenishment reminders, winback, and review request
- Created a subscription-first landing page with a 15% Subscribe & Save offer, reducing friction for repeat buyers
- Set up a weekly automated performance report pulling Meta, Klaviyo, and Shopify data into a single view the founder could review in 10 minutes
- Implemented inventory-based ad pausing to prevent promoting out-of-stock SKUs

**Results:**
- Founder's weekly working hours dropped from 62 to 38 within 8 weeks
- Customer service response time decreased from 14 hours to 45 minutes average
- Email revenue increased 74% after flow rebuild (from $8,200/month to $14,300/month)
- Meta ROAS improved from 1.8x to 3.4x after account restructure -- on the same $2,500/month spend
- Repeat purchase rate reversed from 14% declining to 22% and climbing
- Subscribe & Save enrollment grew from 3% of orders to 11% within 3 months

**Timeline:** 4 months. Operational audit: 1 week. AI automation buildout: 3 weeks. Ad restructure: 2 weeks. Klaviyo rebuild: 1 week. Ongoing optimization through month 4.

**Quote:** "The last agency gave me dashboards I never looked at. SWG gave me 20 hours of my week back and showed me exactly where every dollar was going. I finally feel like I am running the business instead of the business running me."

### Apex Mechanical -- Local Multi-Location Service Business

**Industry:** HVAC and plumbing services (4 locations)
**Revenue Range:** $5.8M annual revenue
**Engagement Duration:** 5 months

**Situation:** Apex Mechanical operates 4 HVAC and plumbing locations across the Dallas-Fort Worth metro. Each location had its own website (built by different vendors over 5 years), its own Google Business Profile, and its own ad hoc way of handling leads -- some used a shared email inbox, one used a whiteboard, and the newest location had a partially configured ServiceTitan instance. The owner had no unified view of which locations were profitable, which marketing was working, or how many leads were being dropped.

**Constraint:** The owner could not shut down operations for a migration. All four locations had to keep running on their existing systems while the new infrastructure was built alongside it. The field technicians (28 total) ranged from tech-comfortable to tech-resistant, and any new system that added friction to their daily workflow would be rejected. The total technology budget was $1,200/month including all software subscriptions.

**Work Performed:**
- Consolidated 4 separate websites into a single multi-location site with location-specific landing pages, service area pages, and unique phone tracking numbers per location
- Implemented ServiceTitan across all 4 locations with standardized lead stages, job categories, and technician assignment workflows
- Migrated 3 years of customer records (11,400 contacts) from spreadsheets, email inboxes, and the partial ServiceTitan instance into a single clean database
- Set up Google Business Profile optimization for all 4 locations: consistent NAP data, service categories, review response templates, and photo uploads
- Built Google Ads campaigns for each service area with location-specific budgets, call-only ads for mobile, and seasonal bid adjustments for HVAC demand cycles
- Created a single-screen operations dashboard showing leads by location, close rate, average ticket, and marketing cost per acquired customer
- Trained 4 office managers on the CRM workflow in 2-hour sessions, with printed quick-reference cards for field techs

**Results:**
- Lead response time dropped from an average of 4.2 hours to 18 minutes across all locations
- Identified that Location 3 was losing money on every Google Ads click due to a misconfigured service area -- fixed within the first week, saving $1,400/month in wasted spend
- Overall lead volume increased 31% in the first 90 days after website consolidation and GBP optimization
- Close rate improved from 34% to 48% after CRM implementation standardized follow-up
- Owner gained real-time visibility into all 4 locations for the first time in the company's history
- Google review count across all locations increased from 187 to 340 in 4 months through automated post-service review requests

**Timeline:** 5 months. Discovery and audit: 2 weeks. Website consolidation: 6 weeks. CRM migration and training: 4 weeks. Google Ads and GBP optimization: 3 weeks. Dashboard and reporting: 2 weeks.

**Quote:** "I had four businesses pretending to be one company. Now I actually have one company with four locations. I can see everything from my phone, and my guys actually use the system because it is simpler than what they had before."

### Archway Labs -- Tech Startup Post-Failed-Agency

**Industry:** B2B SaaS (project management vertical)
**Revenue Range:** $800K ARR
**Engagement Duration:** 3 months

**Situation:** Archway Labs is a 15-person B2B SaaS company selling project management software to construction firms. After closing a $2M seed round, they hired a well-known performance marketing agency at $8,500/month to scale paid acquisition. After 5 months and $42,500 in agency fees (plus $61,000 in ad spend), they had acquired 23 trial signups and zero converted customers from paid channels. The agency blamed "long B2B sales cycles" and recommended increasing budget. The founders fired them and came to SWG with the wreckage.

**Constraint:** The founders were out of patience and nearly out of runway for marketing experimentation. They needed to see directional results within 30 days or they were going to pull all paid spend and rely exclusively on the outbound sales team. The previous agency had left behind a Google Ads account with 847 keywords, 12 campaigns, and no conversion tracking -- everything had to be untangled before anything new could be built.

**Work Performed:**
- Forensic audit of the previous agency's Google Ads account: found broad match keywords bidding on terms like "project management" and "construction software" with no negative keywords, no conversion tracking, and campaigns optimized for clicks rather than conversions
- Discovered the agency had been running traffic to the homepage instead of dedicated landing pages, and the homepage had no clear CTA for the target buyer
- Paused all 12 campaigns, archived 790 irrelevant keywords, and rebuilt from scratch with 3 tightly themed campaigns: Brand Defense (exact match), High-Intent Product (phrase match), and Competitor Comparison (exact match)
- Installed proper conversion tracking: trial signup, demo request, and qualified lead events flowing through to Google Ads and the CRM
- Built 3 dedicated landing pages for construction project management use cases, each with industry-specific social proof, feature callouts, and a single CTA
- Set up a LinkedIn Ads pilot targeting construction company operations managers in the $10M-50M revenue range, with lead gen forms feeding directly into HubSpot
- Created a retargeting program for website visitors and partial trial signups with case-study-driven creative
- Restructured the trial onboarding email sequence from 2 generic emails to a 7-email series with construction-specific templates and use cases

**Results:**
- Cost per trial signup dropped from $2,652 (agency period) to $187 within 45 days
- Google Ads generated 34 qualified trial signups in the first 60 days on $4,200/month spend (vs. 23 in 5 months under the agency at $12,200/month average spend)
- 6 trial users converted to paying customers ($14,400 ARR each) within the 3-month engagement
- LinkedIn pilot generated 18 demo requests at $94 cost per lead
- Trial-to-paid conversion rate increased from 4% to 19% after onboarding email rebuild
- The founders reinstated paid acquisition as a core growth channel and allocated $8,000/month ongoing budget with confidence

**Timeline:** 3 months. Forensic audit and teardown: 1 week. Rebuild and landing pages: 3 weeks. First results: day 30. LinkedIn pilot and retargeting: weeks 5-8. Onboarding email rebuild: week 4. Ongoing optimization through month 3.

**Quote:** "The previous agency had 847 keywords and zero customers. SWG cut it to 57 keywords and we got more signups in two months than we got in five. The difference was someone actually understood our buyer before touching the ad account."
