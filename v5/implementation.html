What SWG builds

Strategy becomes shipped work.

The review identifies what to build. Implementation is where it gets done. Websites, CRM, AI workflows, paid media, dashboards, and operations — scoped and shipped.

Build categories

Eight areas where SWG builds for clients. Each one is scoped from the review, not sold from a menu.

Websites & offers
Full site redesigns, landing pages, offer pages, and conversion-focused storefronts. Built around how customers actually decide, not around what the company wants to say. Shopify, WordPress, or custom — whatever fits the business.
CRM & sales
CRM setup, cleanup, and automation. Lead lifecycle mapping, pipeline structure, handoff rules, and reporting. The goal is a system where the team knows exactly where every prospect stands without asking the founder.
AI workflows
Process automation using AI where the ROI is clear. Content generation pipelines, customer service triage, data extraction, reporting automation, and internal knowledge systems. Built on documented workflows, not speculation.
Marketing execution
Email flows, content systems, SEO foundations, and social strategy. The kind of marketing that compounds over time rather than producing one-off spikes. Integrated with the CRM and analytics from day one.
Paid media
Meta, Google, and TikTok campaign builds tied to real conversion tracking. Creative production, audience strategy, landing page alignment, and reporting. Managed or handed off with documentation.
Social campaigns
Content calendars, creative production, engagement systems, and influencer coordination. Built around the brand voice and measured against business outcomes, not vanity metrics.
Reporting dashboards
Custom dashboards that show the numbers the business actually needs. Revenue by channel, cost of acquisition, pipeline velocity, and campaign performance. Built so the founder can check in five minutes, not fifty.
Operations cleanup
SOPs, role definitions, meeting cadences, and accountability structures. The systems that let a company grow without every decision routing through one person. Boring but load-bearing.

Example build paths

Three real engagement patterns. Each started with the business review and produced shipped work within the first engagement.

D2C supplement brand

From outdated Shopify store to a conversion-focused site with paid media and email flows running within 8 weeks.

The review found the site was converting at 0.8% with no email capture and broken Meta tracking. We rebuilt the product pages, launched Klaviyo flows, fixed CAPI, and deployed three ad sets — all sequenced so each build fed the next.

Site redesign Klaviyo flows Meta ads CAPI tracking

B2B professional services firm

Founder went from 60-hour weeks to a team operating independently within 12 weeks.

The constraint was not marketing. It was that the founder was the operating system. We built SOPs for client onboarding, structured the CRM pipeline, created a reporting dashboard, and defined role ownership — then trained the team to run it.

Operations SOPs CRM pipeline Dashboard Team training

E-commerce brand post-agency

Previous agency left a half-built site and broken analytics. Rebuilt on a sound foundation in 6 weeks.

The review revealed that 40% of the prior build was salvageable. We kept the product data and photography, rebuilt the theme, fixed Google Analytics and conversion tracking, and launched Google Ads on the corrected foundation.

Build rescue Analytics fix Theme rebuild Google Ads
