FAQ

Got Questions? We've Got Answers.

Who is the best fit for SWG?

Founder-led, owner-led, or operator-led companies with real complexity and urgency. If you need both direction and execution across marketing, sales, ops, or technology, we're built for you. We've worked across 29+ industries.

Do we need to know what service we want?

Not at all. We start by understanding your business, not selling a service. The recommendation comes after we understand what's actually blocking growth. That's the whole point of the audit.

What does the roadmap typically include?

It depends on what you need. Could include website redevelopment, brand positioning, marketing strategy, CRM cleanup, AI implementation, workflow design, reporting, content, campaigns, or operating support. Usually it's several of these, connected.

Do you actually build it, or just hand over a plan?

We build it. SWG is strongest when the plan becomes a working system: assets launched, workflows connected, teams trained, campaigns shipped, and reporting reviewed. Strategy without execution is a PDF nobody reads.

How long does an engagement take?

A Business Review is 2-3 weeks. A Focused Build is 4-12 weeks depending on scope. Ongoing Support is month-to-month with no lock-in. We scale with you.

What kind of ROI can we expect?

Our clients see an average 6x ROI. We measure real business outcomes, not vanity metrics like impressions or clicks. 94% of our clients stay with us because the results compound over time.
