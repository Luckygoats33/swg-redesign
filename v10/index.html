01 Problems We Solve

Six ways growing companies quietly bleed money

Most founders don't have a growth problem. They have one of these six problems wearing a growth problem's clothes.

No. 1

Fragmented marketing

Ads in one place, email in another, a freelancer running social. Nothing shares data, nothing compounds, and nobody owns the outcome.

No. 2

No real CRM

Leads live in inboxes, spreadsheets, and someone's memory. Follow-up depends on who remembered — and deals die in the gaps.

No. 3

The founder bottleneck

Every sale, every decision, every fire runs through you. The business grows exactly as fast as your calendar allows — and no faster.

No. 4

Wasted ad spend

Budget goes out, "impressions" come back. Misconfigured targeting, bloated keywords, and no tracking that ties spend to revenue.

No. 5

No reporting you trust

You're making six-figure decisions off gut feel because the dashboards contradict each other — or don't exist at all.

No. 6

Disconnected systems

Website, store, CRM, email, accounting — nine tools, zero handshakes. Your team re-types data all day and customers feel the seams.
