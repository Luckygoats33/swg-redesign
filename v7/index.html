The core problem

Growth stalls when systems are disconnected.

Most companies past $1M have the pieces: paid media, a CRM, a website, maybe some AI tools. Each one was built to solve a specific problem at a specific moment.

But nobody connected them. The ad spend drives traffic to a website that doesn't convert. The CRM captures leads but doesn't nurture them. Reporting looks backward while the business moves forward.

Growth leaks through the gaps between systems. SWG finds the gaps, sequences the fixes, and builds the connective tissue that turns separate tools into one growth engine.

How we work

Diagnose. Sequence. Build.

Three phases, one connected outcome. Every engagement follows the same discipline.

01 Diagnose

We audit your full stack — website, marketing, CRM, operations, reporting — to find where growth is leaking. Not a surface-level review. A forensic look at how your systems connect and where they don't.

02 Sequence

Most companies try to fix everything at once. We build a prioritized roadmap that sequences fixes by impact — what to build first, what to build next, and what to leave alone for now.

03 Build

We build it. Website, email flows, ad campaigns, AI automations, dashboards — all connected to the strategy. No handoff to another vendor. Same team, end to end.
